Privacy Policy

Draft — have this reviewed before relying on it for compliance.

Last updated: 2026-08-17

Overview

AIVis ("we", "us") is a small, self-serve tool that checks whether AI search tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok mention your business, and tracks that over time. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address, used for login and account identification via Neon Auth.
  • Company data you enter — brand name, website, category, competitors, use case, region, and customer segment for each company you add. This is the data used to run scans; you control what's entered.
  • Scan results — the AI models' responses to our prompts, the resulting visibility score, competitor tallies, and any advice generated from that scan.
  • Payment information — if you upgrade to a paid plan, Stripe collects and processes your card details directly. AIVis never sees or stores your card number; we only receive a subscription/payment status from Stripe.

How we use it

  • Running the AI-visibility scans you request, which involves sending your company/category/competitor data to AI models to generate grounded, web-search-backed answers — one model (GPT-5 mini) via Perplexity's grounded search API, and three (Gemini, Claude, Grok) sent directly to their own providers' APIs.
  • Computing and displaying your visibility score and scan history.
  • Billing and subscription management, handled through Stripe.
  • Operating, maintaining, and improving the product — for example, diagnosing a failed scan.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train AI models of our own.

Who we share it with

These service providers process data on our behalf, only as needed to run AIVis:

  • Perplexity AI — receives the prompts built from your company data to run a grounded search against GPT-5 mini. This is one of the four AI checks that make up a scan.
  • Anthropic, Google, and xAI — each receives the prompts built from your company data directly (not through Perplexity) to run a grounded search against their own model — Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 3 Flash, and Grok 4.6 respectively. Together with Perplexity above, these are the four AI checks that make up a scan.
  • Stripe — handles all payment processing for paid plans. AIVis never stores your card number or full payment details.
  • Neon — hosts our database and authentication (login/session) system.
  • Netlify — hosts the application and runs its backend functions.

We don't share your data with anyone else, and never for advertising purposes.

Data retention

We keep your account and company/scan data for as long as your account is active, so you can see your visibility trend over time. If you want your account and its data deleted, contact us (below) and we'll remove it, aside from records we're legally required to keep (e.g. billing records for tax purposes).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) between your browser, our servers, and our providers. Access to the underlying database is restricted to what the application needs to function. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your data.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account entirely at any time by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of AIVis after a change means you accept the updated policy.
