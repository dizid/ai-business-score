Ask an AI model "what's the best AI-visibility tool" or "what's the best
GEO platform for tracking brand mentions" and you don't get an empty
answer. You get a shortlist — usually the same handful of names, in some
order, with a sentence of reasoning attached to each. That's worth sitting
with for a second: this is already a populated category. Nobody claiming a
GEO tool is "the best" is claiming uncontested ground.

So here's the honest version of that shortlist, and where a tool like
Foreground actually sits in it — not the version optimized to make one
answer sound biggest.

## The tools people actually name

**Profound** is the one enterprise teams tend to land on. It tracks
citations across ten or more engines, adds "agent analytics" to show how
AI crawlers are actually reading your site, and is built for teams that
need compliance-grade reporting, not a quick check. Entry pricing starts
around $99/month, with custom quotes above that for larger accounts.

**Otterly.AI** is the budget end of the same idea — daily brand-mention
tracking across the major models, aimed at solo operators and small
teams who want a number to watch without a five-figure commitment.
Entry pricing sits around $29/month.

**Peec AI** occupies the middle: European-built, growing fast, focused on
share-of-voice reporting against named competitors across models. Entry
pricing starts around $95/month.

**Scrunch AI** takes a different angle from the others — instead of just
telling you whether you're cited, it's built around fixing the content
that isn't getting cited, with legibility audits and multi-LLM monitoring
aimed at content-heavy teams. Pricing is custom/enterprise.

**Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit** is the option for anyone already
paying for Semrush — AI-citation tracking bundled into an existing SEO
subscription rather than a new line item.

**ZipTie.dev** also comes up in this conversation, positioned around an
"AI Success Score" and agency-facing reporting — worth knowing the name
exists, though I haven't used it myself and won't guess at its pricing
here.

## Where Foreground actually fits

Five models, queried directly against their own provider APIs — GPT-5
mini, Gemini 3 Flash, Claude Haiku 4.5, Grok 4.6, and Mistral Small — kept
cheap enough to give away three full scans free, no card required. That's
the actual trade-off against Profound's ten-plus engines: fewer data
points per scan, but a genuinely lower floor to try it, and a real free
tier instead of a sales call.

If you run one local or small-business brand and want a plain "does AI
mention me" answer without committing to a monitoring subscription,
that's the gap Foreground sits in.

## When you'd want something else

If you need Google AI Overviews coverage specifically, ten or more
engines tracked simultaneously, or agency/white-label reporting for
multiple client accounts, Foreground isn't built for that today — Profound
or Scrunch are the better fit, and I'd rather say that here than have you
find it out after paying for the wrong tool. Foreground's own
[limitations are stated plainly on the how-it-works page](/how-it-works) —
worth reading before you buy anything, mine included.
