FAQ

What is AI search visibility?

It's whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI tools mention your business when someone asks a question in your category — the same idea as SEO, but for a single generated answer instead of a page of links you could scan for your name.

How is the score calculated?

Foreground runs category-relevant prompts through multiple AI models and checks whether your business is mentioned, how it ranks against competitors, and how often. The 0-100 score reflects how consistently you show up first versus not at all.

Which AI models does it check?

OpenAI's GPT-5 mini, Google's Gemini 3 Flash, Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5, and xAI's Grok 4.6 — GPT-5 mini queried through Perplexity's grounded search API, the other three queried directly against each provider's own API, so responses reflect real web results, not just each model's static training data.

What does it cost?

Free to try — one company and three scans, no credit card required. Just need one check? A single scan is $19, one-time, no account required. Pro is $199/month for unlimited companies, 20 scans a month (with top-up packs available), and unlimited AI-generated deep advice.
