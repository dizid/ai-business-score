FAQ

What is AI search visibility?

It's whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI tools mention your business when someone asks a question in your category — the same idea as SEO, but for a single generated answer instead of a page of links you could scan for your name.

How is the score calculated?

Foreground runs category-relevant prompts through multiple AI models and checks whether your business is mentioned, how it ranks against competitors, and how often. The 0-100 score reflects how consistently you show up first versus not at all.

Which AI models does it check?

OpenAI's GPT-5 mini, Google's Gemini 3 Flash, Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5, and xAI's Grok 4.6 — each queried directly against its own provider's API, so responses reflect real web results, not just each model's static training data.
