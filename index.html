FAQ

What is AI search visibility?

It's whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI tools mention your business when someone asks a question in your category — the same idea as SEO, but for a single generated answer instead of a page of links you could scan for your name.

How is the score calculated?

AIVis runs category-relevant prompts through multiple AI models and checks whether your business is mentioned, how it ranks against competitors, and how often. The 0-100 score reflects how consistently you show up first versus not at all.

Which AI models does it check?

OpenAI's GPT-5 mini, Google's Gemini 3 Flash, Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5, and xAI's Grok 4.6 — all four queried through Perplexity's grounded search API so responses reflect real web results, not just each model's static training data.

What does it cost?

Free to try — one company and three scans, no credit card required. A paid plan unlocks unlimited companies and scans for a single flat monthly price.
