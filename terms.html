2. What Foreground does

Foreground checks whether AI search tools (currently OpenAI's GPT-5 mini, Google's Gemini 3 Flash, Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5, and xAI's Grok 4.6 — GPT-5 mini queried through Perplexity's grounded search API, the other three queried directly against each provider's own API) mention a business when asked relevant category questions, and reports the result as a score you can track over time. See How this works for the full explanation of the methodology.

Use scores and advice as a directional signal to guide your own AI-search optimization work, not as a certified or warrantied measurement.
