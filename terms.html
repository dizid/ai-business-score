2. What AIVis does

AIVis checks whether AI search tools (currently OpenAI's GPT-5 mini, Google's Gemini 3 Flash, Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5, and xAI's Grok 4.6 — GPT-5 mini queried through Perplexity's grounded search API, the other three queried directly against each provider's own API) mention a business when asked relevant category questions, and reports the result as a score you can track over time. See How this works for the full explanation of the methodology.

4. Scores are informational, not guaranteed

AIVis's detection is presence-only, not sentiment-aware — a negative or comparative mention of your business still counts as "cited." The visibility score is a heuristic weighting of that same presence-only detection, not an independently verified ranking, and each scan is a single point-in-time check of live AI model outputs that AIVis doesn't control and can't guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or consistency of. Use scores and advice as a directional signal to guide your own AI-search optimization work, not as a certified or warrantied measurement. Any advice generated (including AI-generated "deeper advice") is provided as-is and should be evaluated using your own judgment before you act on it.
